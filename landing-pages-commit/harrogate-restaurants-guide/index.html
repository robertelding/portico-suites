How Locals Navigate It

Head for the Montpellier Quarter for the bistro-and-wine-bar heart of town; Cold Bath Road for neighbourhood favourites; the centre for everything from Italian stalwarts to modern Yorkshire menus. Bettys remains the ceremony — queue for breakfast or book the Imperial Room experience ahead.

Groups of ten should book ahead everywhere at weekends. And when the town is full or the mood is home-cooked, the house's own oak table seats the party with a kitchen to match — private chefs and caterers also serve the area.
