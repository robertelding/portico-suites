Things To Do in Harrogate

England's original spa town rewards a slow weekend or a working week's evenings alike. Here is the shortlist we give every guest — all of it close to the house.

Local Guide

In Town — All Walkable

  • Bettys Café Tea Rooms — the institution; go early or queue happily
  • The Turkish Baths — restored Victorian baths, book a session ahead
  • Valley Gardens — gardens, play areas and the boating-lake path
  • Montpellier Quarter — independent shops, galleries and restaurants
  • RHS Harlow Carr — one of the great northern gardens, with a Bettys of its own
Local Guide

Easy Days Out

  • Knaresborough — riverside rowing boats beneath the viaduct, castle ruins above (10 minutes)
  • Fountains Abbey & Studley Royal — World Heritage abbey ruins and water gardens
  • Ripley Castle & village — a compact, charming half-day
  • Harewood House — grand state rooms, gardens and bird garden between Harrogate and Leeds
  • The Yorkshire Dales — Nidderdale and Wharfedale begin on Harrogate's doorstep
  • York — direct trains make it an easy day trip

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

What is Harrogate best known for?
Its spa-town heritage — the Turkish Baths, Valley Gardens and Montpellier Quarter — plus Bettys tea rooms and access to the Yorkshire Dales.
What can families do in Harrogate?
The Valley Gardens, Knaresborough's boats and castle, RHS Harlow Carr and easy Dales walks all suit children well.
Do I need a car?
Not for the town itself — everything central is walkable from Portico Suites, and trains run direct to York, Leeds and Knaresborough.
